Audio note: This version uses offline browser audio processing and exports a WAV file. Pitch shifting is done with resampling, so lower pitches may sound slightly longer or more synthetic than a professional pitch shifter. Voice recordings stay on your device; this page does not upload them anywhere.

For best results, use a clear recording with little background noise. Microphone recording requires permission and usually works on HTTPS or localhost; loading an audio file works from a local HTML file in modern browsers.
